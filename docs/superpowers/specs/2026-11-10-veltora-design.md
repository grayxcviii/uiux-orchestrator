# VELTORA Noir — luxury perfume campaign

## Intent
The user's latest direction replaces the abstract chrome campaign with luxury perfume marketing, retaining the VELTORA brand and smooth animation.

## Design
A realistic amber perfume bottle with thick glass, glossy black cap, ivory VELTORA / NOIR label and champagne lighting on dark stone. Elegant serif typography, warm ivory, espresso background and ample negative space. Hero: “Some things stay with you.” Second scene: “An impression. Not an echo.” with illustrative fragrance notes.

## Approach
Built-in image generation creates the product photograph; local HTML/CSS composes the campaign. A deterministic ten-second photographic pan/zoom and exposure timeline animates the composition. This is not a rotating 3D model. The exact generation prompt and provenance are retained beside the source.

## Constraints
Keep sources and generated product photograph in the repository. No external runtime dependencies. Responsive desktop/mobile composition and reduced-motion still. Export JPEG, WebP, GIF and MP4. Identify the brand, scent and specifications as fictional and illustrative. Preserve unrelated edits and historical Vahl evidence. No commit, push or publish requested.

## Verification
Inspect hero, fragrance composition, tablet and mobile captures. Verify image loading, controls, reduced motion, exact loop closure, script syntax and exported media duration/dimensions.
